# PVE Markets: How Community-Owned Liquidity Compounds

A prospective Build In Public University course for learning how a market mechanism becomes a community practice.

Case study: public descriptions of Long, tokenized-stock pairs, community vaults, liquidity, and the PVE/PVP distinction.

Status: prospective course / local learner trial / not investment advice

## The loop

```text
concept → question → bounded assignment → receipt → next concept
```

This course does not ask a learner to buy, trade, promote, or connect a wallet. It asks the learner to understand a mechanism well enough to name its inputs, outputs, evidence, and failure conditions.

## Open the course web

[Enter the networked lesson](game.html) — the playable map of concepts.

[Start at the course hub](index.html) if you want the plain course overview.

The game is a local-first graph. Visiting a concept marks that node as visited in browser `localStorage`; adjacent nodes open as routes. Forging Assignment 01 unlocks the Synthesis node. Resetting the run clears only that browser’s local state.

The pages are deliberately static and forkable. The assignment receipt is generated in the browser and stays local unless the learner chooses to share it.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, a learner should be able to:

1. Explain what a stock-token pair is doing as a coordination object.
2. Distinguish positive-sum participation claims from PVP extraction claims.
3. Draw a compounding loop without hiding its assumptions.
4. Identify who controls a reserve, who benefits, and what remains unverified.
5. Separate liquidity, volume, attention, ownership, and usage as different measurements.
6. Write one evidence-backed claim and one falsifier.
7. Produce a proof-of-work receipt without making an investment recommendation.

## Course boundary

Long is used as a public case study, not as a course sponsor or endorsement. Posts, dashboards, and product pages are evidence sources to inspect, not truths to inherit. Financial claims require independent verification. The course is educational and does not provide investment, tax, legal, or trading advice.

The local receipt contains learner-entered text. Do not include private keys, wallet addresses, personal data, or confidential information.
