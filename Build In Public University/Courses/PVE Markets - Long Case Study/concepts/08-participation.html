CONCEPT 08 · CASE STUDY

Participation is not investment

How can you learn without being recruited?

The learner needs a way to inspect a market system without being turned into its exit liquidity. BIPU treats the mechanism as a case study: read, map, verify, question, and publish a falsifier. The receipt is proof of work, not proof of returns.

Make the mechanism smaller.

Describe the system in a way that remains useful even if the asset price goes to zero. If the explanation collapses, it was probably a promotion.

Falsifier to carry forward: What part of your understanding survives a negative market outcome?
