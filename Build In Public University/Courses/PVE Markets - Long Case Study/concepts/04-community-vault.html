CONCEPT 04 · CASE STUDY

Community vaults

Who owns the reserve and who can change it?

A community vault changes the question from “what did the protocol earn?” to “what does the community-controlled reserve contain, and what rules govern it?” Ownership, custody, control, and benefit are separate claims.

TRY THIS

Make the mechanism smaller.

For any reserve, identify the asset, custodian, controller, rule for accumulation, rule for withdrawal, and beneficiary.

Falsifier to carry forward: A public balance is not proof of community control. What receipt would show who can move it?
