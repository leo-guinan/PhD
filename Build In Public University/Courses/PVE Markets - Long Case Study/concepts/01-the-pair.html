CONCEPT 01 · CASE STUDY

The pair

What is being connected, and why?

A stock-token pair connects a crypto-native coordination layer to an underlying stock reference. The point is not that the two assets become identical. The point is that attention, liquidity, and participation can move between them.

TRY THIS

Make the mechanism smaller.

Start by naming both sides. What does each side contribute? What does the pair make possible that either side alone does not?

Falsifier to carry forward: When a pair is described as “stock-backed,” ask: backed how, by whom, through which contract, and with what observable receipt?
