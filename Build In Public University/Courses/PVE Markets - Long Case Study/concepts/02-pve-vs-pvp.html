PVE vs PVP

What behavior is the design trying to reward?

PVP describes a game where participants mainly extract from one another. PVE describes a design that tries to make participant cooperation increase the size, depth, or durability of the shared field. The label is a hypothesis about incentives, not a moral certificate.
