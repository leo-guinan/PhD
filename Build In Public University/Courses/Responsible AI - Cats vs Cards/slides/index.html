Responsible conversational AI

AI systems are cats.
Responsible products use cards.

When the model is capable, persuasive, and occasionally wrong, the product needs visible boundaries around it.

01 / 09
01 / The operating metaphor

The cat is the model.
The cards are the product.

CAT

Probabilistic, adaptive, hard to fully specify

It can be useful without being reliable. It can follow the sentence while missing the situation. It does not become safe because the system prompt sounded stern.

CARD

Explicit, bounded, inspectable

A card states what can happen, what must not happen, who approves the next step, and what evidence gets recorded.

02 / 09
02 / Why demos lie

A good conversation is not a safety result.

The demo asks: “Can the system produce a convincing answer?” Responsible evaluation asks: “What happens when the user is confused, adversarial, vulnerable, rushed, or simply wrong?”

03 / 09
03 / Put four cards around the cat

Every consequential capability needs a boundary.

CAPABILITYWhat can the system do?
ESCALATIONWhen must a person take over?
RECEIPTWhat evidence survives the turn?
FALSIFIERWhat would make this design fail?

The cards are not decoration. They are the control surface around a system whose internal reasoning you cannot reliably inspect.

04 / 09
04 / Failure-oriented evals

Test the conversations you hope nobody starts.

Build the eval set from failure modes

  • Confidently wrong answers
  • Requests for private information
  • Emotional dependency cues
  • Instruction conflicts and prompt injection
  • Requests that cross an action boundary
A TEST CASE IS A CARD

Setup: user asks for an irreversible action while presenting ambiguous authority.

Expected: system pauses, states the uncertainty, asks for confirmation or escalates.

Receipt: input class, response, intervention, outcome.

05 / Human control

Keep the human at the action boundary, not in the marketing copy.

Conversation
model proposes
→
Decision
system classifies
→
Approval
person confirms
→
Action
external effect

“Human in the loop” is not a person who can theoretically intervene. It is a specific stop, a specific authority, and a specific record of whether intervention happened.

06 / 09
06 / Receipts

The thesis means nothing without the artifact.

A receipt answers the boring questions that become important after the system is wrong.

Minimum receipt

  • What did the user ask?
  • What did the system infer?
  • What did it do or refuse to do?
  • Who intervened?
  • What happened next?

A log is not automatically an audit. Preserve provenance, define the outcome, and keep “generated” separate from “acted.”

07 / 09
07 / Five-minute exercise

Choose one cat. Draw its cards.

1

Name the capability

What can the assistant actually change outside the chat?

2

Name the failure

What would a motivated or vulnerable user make it do?

3

Name the receipt

What evidence would let you reconstruct the incident?

If the team cannot answer those three questions, the system is not ready for more autonomy. It is ready for a smaller demo.

08 / 09
08 / The operating rule

Test the cat.
Strengthen the cards.
Publish the miss.

Responsible conversational AI is not the absence of failure. It is a system that makes failure harder to cause, easier to detect, safer to contain, and impossible to quietly rewrite.
